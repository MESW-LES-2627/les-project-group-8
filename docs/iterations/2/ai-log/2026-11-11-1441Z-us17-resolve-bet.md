# Resolve a Bet by choosing its winner (US17)

- **Who:** João Luiz Cerqueira.
- **Tools:** Cursor (Grok 4.6).
- **Outcome:** [PR #89](https://github.com/MESW-LES-2627/les-project-group-8/pull/89)
  for [#76](https://github.com/MESW-LES-2627/les-project-group-8/issues/76):
  store a Bet's winning Outcome and show it on the Pool.
- **Delegated:** implementing `POST /api/pools/{pool_id}/bets/{bet_id}/resolve`,
  the resolve UI, acceptance tests for ACs 17.1–17.5, and marking US17 Done.
- **What we decided ourselves, and why:** resolve per Bet, never per Pool;
  US18–US20 stay out of this slice. The resolve control is its own component,
  mounted from `BetCard.svelte` with one line.
- **How we checked:** backend tests and frontend checks on the PR; teammate
  review before merge.
