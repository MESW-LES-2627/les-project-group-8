# MVP

A web application where friends create prediction Pools containing related Bets (Questions).Each person has their own Account and chooses one Outcome per Bet before the Pool's shared deadline. Once the events occur, the creator records the winning Outcomes. Participants can compare their predictions and track their wins and losses on a personal scoreboard. The pool creator is able to see all answers and broader statistics.

This is the scope we propose to deliver by the end of the course. The
[vision](i02.d001-vision.md) describes the broader product, while the
[user stories](i02.d003-user-stories.md) detail the MVP requirements.

## Who is it for?

Groups of friends who create casual, zero-money prediction pools on real-life events (sports, reality TV, personal bets) and want a single place to settle who was right and track their win/loss record.

## MVP Outcomes (What the user can do)

1. **Have an Account.** Sign up with an email, password and display name, log in
  and log out. Pools and Picks belong to the person using that Account.
2. **Make a Pool.** Set its name, optional description and shared deadline, then
  add Bets. Each Bet has a statement and 2–10 distinct, ordered Outcomes,  presented as radio buttons, multiple selection, dropdown or open ended.
3. **Join a Pool.** Open a shared link and read its details without signing in;
  log in to participate with your own Picks.
4. **Make and change Picks.** Choose one Outcome per Bet, revisit your saved
  choices and change them before the deadline. Other participants' Picks stay
   hidden until picking closes.
5. **Resolve Bets.** The creator marks each Bet's winning Outcome after the
  deadline and the event, allowing participants to compare their predictions.
6. **Track your score.** See your wins and losses from resolved Bets on a
  personal scoreboard, and compete with your friends results.



### Core User Journey (MVP Path)

1. **User A** signs up or logs in and creates an empty Pool with a name,
  optional description and future deadline.
2. **User A** adds related Bets and their possible Outcomes, then shares the
  Pool link. Only the creator can add Bets, and only before the deadline.
3. **Friends** open the link, read the Bets, log in and save their Picks. Each
  person can change a Pick without changing their answers to other Bets, and
   their saved choices remain available when they return.
4. When the deadline arrives, adding Bets and making or changing Picks closes.
  Participants can see what everyone picked and compare predictions.
5. Once each event concludes, **User A** selects that Bet's winning Outcome.
6. The resolved Bets show their results and who picked correctly. Participants'
  personal scoreboards reflect their wins and losses.

## Definition of Done

A task or user story is considered Done when:

1. **Runs on the commit or tag & deployed environment**: The change runs on its
  identified commit or tag and works in the deployed app, not just locally.
2. **Automated Testing & CI**: Its acceptance criteria are satisfied and covered
  by tests, and all required automated tests, frontend checks and CI checks pass.
3. **Specification & Architecture Aligned**: The change follows the
  [API contract](i02.d007.openapi.yaml) and architecture, and affected
   [user stories](i02.d003-user-stories.md) and documentation remain consistent.
