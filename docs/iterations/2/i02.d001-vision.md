# Vision

PoolWithFriends is a place for friends to turn predictions into shared
competitions, without money. People create Pools around any event, make Picks,
compare results and follow their performance over time. Unlike products tied
to a particular sport or topic, it supports anything from a single match or
awards ceremony to a competition spanning an entire season.

This is the vision for the complete product.

## Who is it for?

Friends and organizers who want to run prediction games for their groups,
adapt the experience to their interests and keep participation, answers,
results and scores together in one place.

## Pools, templates and rounds

An organizer can create an empty Pool and build it around their event, choosing
its name, description, cover images and visual theme. Friends can discover
what the Pool is about through its shared link and read its Bets before
deciding to participate.

Pools support both one-off and recurring events. A one-off Pool has one Round;
a recurring Pool has ordered, named Rounds, each containing its own Bets and
prediction deadline. Participants can follow the whole competition while
distinguishing each Round's choices, results and scores.

Templates offer reusable starting points, such as a Premier League season or
the Oscars, with predefined Rounds and Bets. Creating a Pool from a Template
copies that structure. The organizer can then customize their Pool
independently, without changing the Template or other Pools created from it.

## Accounts, participation and Bets

Each person has their own Account and display name, can log in and out, and
can return to their saved Picks. Joining a Pool establishes one participation
for that User in that Pool. Organizers can also participate in their own Pools.

Organizers can combine several types of Bet within a Round:

- **Multiple choice:** one answer from two or more ordered Outcomes, displayed
  as radio buttons or a dropdown.
- **Open-ended:** a free-text answer when possible responses cannot be
  usefully enumerated.
- **Numerical:** a number with an appropriate unit, such as goals or minutes.
- **List selection:** several Outcomes from a list, with minimum and maximum
  numbers of selections defined for the Bet.

Each participant has one Pick per Bet, containing the answer appropriate to
its type. They can review or change it before the Round's deadline without
affecting their other Picks. Once picking closes, answers remain available
for comparison. Other participants' Picks stay hidden before the deadline
so people can make their own predictions.

## Results, scores and statistics

The organizer records each Bet's result after its event and deadline. A result
can identify winning Outcomes, a numerical value or a text answer, according
to the Bet's type. If the organizer corrects a result, the affected scores and
rankings are recalculated.

Bets can carry different maximum points, allowing organizers to give more
weight to some predictions. Participants can see their wins, losses and earned
points on personal scoreboards, alongside leaderboards for the whole Pool or
an individual Round. Rankings show both current positions and position changes
as the competition progresses.

Statistics help participants understand their accuracy and performance across
Rounds. Organizers can review participation and answers, see how frequently
each response was chosen, and compare predictions with recorded results.
Together, these views make the Pool useful throughout the competition and
provide a record the group can revisit afterwards.
