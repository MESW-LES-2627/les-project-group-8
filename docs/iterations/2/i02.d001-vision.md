# PoolWithFriends — Product Vision

## Vision

Bring friends closer through playful competition around the events they love.

## Product Vision Board

| Field              | Our direction                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Target group**   | Groups of friends who enjoy predicting sports, entertainment or everyday events. **Organizers** create and manage competitions; **participants** make Picks and compare their Records. Organizers can also participate.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Needs**          | <ins>**Make shared events more fun through friendly competition.**</ins> Friends can put their predictions to the test and enjoy finding out who was right. **Compare predictions fairly:** Picks are recorded before the deadline, so everyone can see what was chosen and how it turned out. **Spend less effort organizing:** the organizer can keep Picks and results together instead of chasing messages and manually tallying answers.                     |
| **Product**        | A web application with four key capabilities: **(1)** friend-created Pools around events of their choice; **(2)** participation through a shared link; **(3)** editable Picks before a deadline, hidden from other participants until picking closes; **(4)** creator-recorded results and comparable win/loss Records. Its focus is flexible, zero-money competition within existing friend groups. **Feasibility:** We have already presented a working vertical slice. |
| **Business goals** | **Primary goal:** establish repeat use among friend groups, providing evidence that the product merits continued development. Monetization is outside the course release.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

## Stakeholders

- **Participants:** want enjoyable competitions, preserved Picks and clear, trustworthy results.
- **Organizers:** want to set up Pools, manage participation and record results with little effort.
- **Development team:** builds and maintains the product, agrees on scope and checks whether it meets user needs.

## Horizon and relationship to the MVP

The long-term ambition is for prediction competitions to become a recurring part of how friends enjoy shared interests, with Pools covering anything from sports and contests to movies, video games and everyday events.

By the end of the course, the [MVP](i02.d002-mvp.md) should support the complete journey: create a Pool containing Bets, invite friends, make Picks before the deadline, record winning Outcomes and compare Records. Accounts preserve each person's identity and choices.

Templates, multiple Rounds and richer statistics are possible future developments if we extend the product beyond the MVP.

## Non-goals

**Product boundary:** no real-money stakes, payments, odds or monetary prizes.

Out of scope for the MVP:

- Reusable Templates and competitions structured into multiple Rounds.
- Numerical, free-text or multiple-selection answers; each Pick selects one predefined Outcome per Bet.
- Weighted scoring, custom rewards and advanced performance analytics.
- Separate third-party referees; the Pool creator records winning Outcomes.
