# PoolWithFriends — Product Vision

## Product Vision Board

### Vision

> [What is the reason for creating the product? What positive change should it create?][pvb]

- Bring friends closer through playful competition around the events they love.

### Target group

> [Which market or market segment does the product address? Who are the target customers and users?][pvb]

- Groups of friends who enjoy making Picks on sports, entertainment or everyday events.
- The Pool's creator sets up and manages the Pool, and is its Decider; the creator can also make Picks.
- Users make Picks and compare their Records.

### Needs

> [What problem does the product solve or which benefit does it offer? If you identify several needs, prioritise them and move the most important one to the top.][pvb]

1. Make shared events more fun through friendly competition: friends put their Picks to the test and enjoy finding out who was right.
2. Compare Picks fairly: Picks are recorded before the deadline, so everyone can see what was chosen and how it turned out.
3. Spend less effort organizing: the Pool's creator keeps Picks and Resolutions together instead of chasing messages and tallying answers by hand.

### Product

> [What product is it? What are its three to five stand-out features that set it apart from competing offering? Is it feasible to develop the product?][pvb]

- A web application for flexible, zero-money competition within existing friend groups, with four key capabilities:
  1. Pools that friends create around events of their choice.
  2. Joining through a shared link.
  3. Picks that can be changed before the deadline and stay hidden from other Users until picking closes.
  4. Resolutions by the Decider, and a clear, trustworthy Leaderboard of win/loss Records.
- Feasibility: we have already presented a working vertical slice, the [Iteration 1 release](https://github.com/MESW-LES-2627/les-project-group-8/releases/tag/iter-1).

### Business goals

> [How will the product benefit the company that develops and provides it? What are the desired business benefits? Prioritise them and move the most important one to the top.][pvb]

- Primary goal: establish repeat use among friend groups, providing evidence that the product merits continued development.
- Monetization is outside the course release.

## Stakeholders

- **Users:** want enjoyable competitions, preserved Picks and a clear, trustworthy Leaderboard.
- **The Pool's creator:** wants to set up Pools, manage who takes part and resolve Bets with little effort.
- **Development team:** builds and maintains the product, agrees on scope and checks whether it meets user needs.

## Horizon and relationship to the MVP

The long-term ambition is for Pools to become a recurring part of how friends enjoy shared interests, covering anything from sports and contests to movies, video games and everyday events.

By the end of the course, the [MVP](i03.d002-mvp.md) should support the complete journey: create a Pool containing Bets, invite friends, make Picks before the deadline, resolve Bets and compare Records. Accounts preserve each person's identity and choices.

Templates, multiple rounds and richer statistics are possible future developments if we extend the product beyond the MVP.

## Non-goals

**Product boundary:** no real-money stakes, payments, odds or monetary prizes.

Out of scope for the MVP:

- Reusable templates and competitions structured into multiple rounds.
- Bets worth different amounts, custom rewards and advanced performance analytics.
- A Decider other than the Pool's creator.
